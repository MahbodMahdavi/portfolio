# Retail Sales Executive Dashboard

## Overview

The **Retail Sales Executive Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI report designed to transform retail sales data into actionable business insights.

Built using Microsoft Power BI, the dashboard provides executives and business stakeholders with an intuitive view of sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, and regional performance through dynamic visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs).

This project demonstrates an end-to-end business intelligence workflow, including data preparation, data modeling, DAX calculations, and dashboard development.

---

## Business Problem

Retail organizations generate large volumes of transactional data, making it difficult to quickly identify trends and make informed decisions.

This dashboard addresses that challenge by providing a centralized view of business performance, enabling stakeholders to monitor key metrics, identify high-performing products, analyze customer purchasing patterns, and evaluate regional sales performance.

---

## Objectives

- Monitor overall business performance
- Track revenue and order trends
- Identify top-performing products
